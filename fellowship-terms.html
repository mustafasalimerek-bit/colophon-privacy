Fellowship is optional

Who can join

Fellowship is for adults: 18 or older (19 or older in Korea). It asks for your birth year, which can’t be changed later, and opens on 1 January of the year you turn 19. Don’t use Fellowship if you are younger, if we have banned you before, or if the law where you live doesn’t allow you to.

What you write

  • It stays yours. You keep the rights you have in your prayer requests, comments and messages.
  • You let us show it. So that Fellowship can work, you give us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to store, copy, check, show and send what you post to the members you share it with, for as long as it is kept under the privacy policy. The check includes the automated AI review, which may replace other people’s names and places with general words. We never use what you write for advertising and never sell it.

  • You are responsible for it. Don’t post anything you don’t have the right to share, including other people’s private stories.
  • It can be removed. We may remove anything that breaks these terms or the community rules, or that puts someone at risk.

Using Fellowship

The community rules are part of these terms: they say what is and isn’t allowed. In addition, don’t use bots or scripts to use Fellowship; don’t try to get around a limit, a block, a suspension or a ban, including by opening another account; and don’t try to break, overload or misuse the service.

Moderation, suspension and closing accounts

Prayer requests, comments and messages are checked by automatic filters and by AI, and reports are reviewed, as the community rules describe. We may remove content, or suspend or ban an account, with or without warning, when these terms or the community rules are broken, to protect someone, or when the law requires it. A ban stays attached to the Apple ID or Google account, so deleting the account is not a way back in.

Only you can cancel Chat+, in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play › Subscriptions: a suspension, a ban or deleting your account doesn’t cancel it. You can stop using Fellowship at any time.

How long things are kept

  • Prayer requests and their comments are deleted from the server 30 days after the request was posted. You can delete your own request or comment at any time; its text is removed at once.
  • The server deletes the text of room messages and private messages 180 days after they were sent.
  • When something is reported, the copy kept for safety review is deleted within 365 days; a copy of a prayer request or comment goes with the request, after 30 days.

The privacy policy has the details.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Fellowship account at any time in Fellowship’s settings, under Delete chat account. Deletion is immediate: your Fellowship profile, prayer requests, comments, messages and settings are deleted and can’t be restored, apart from the report copies and the ban or age block that the privacy policy describes. Nothing else in Colophon changes: your Bible reading, notes and Colophon Pro stay as they are. Deleting the account, or the app, doesn’t cancel Chat+; cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play › Subscriptions. If you can’t open the app, write to us and we’ll delete the account for you: Deleting your Fellowship account says how.

Chat+

Chat+ is an optional, auto-renewing subscription sold in Fellowship: $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year in the United States. Prices in other countries may differ; the App Store or Google Play shows your price before you buy. While it is active, Chat+ includes exactly this:

  • posting prayer requests on the Prayer Wall, up to 10 a week;
  • unlimited messages in the Fellowship room;
  • unlimited private messages, sending and reading;
  • every Colophon Pro feature, for as long as the subscription is active.

Reading the Prayer Wall and the room, commenting, “I prayed” and every safety tool are free for everyone. Without Chat+, room and private messages can have daily limits; the app shows them when they apply. “Unlimited” means no daily limit — the community rules and the ordinary limits against spam still apply.

Payment and renewal on iPhone and iPad. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. Chat+ renews automatically at the same price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple ID settings (on iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions). After you cancel, Chat+ stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Payment and renewal on Android. Chat+ is billed by Google Play. Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. Chat+ renews automatically at the same price until you cancel it in Google Play › Subscriptions (Fellowship’s settings link there under “Manage subscription”). After you cancel, Chat+ stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds are handled by the store under its own policies, not by us: on iPhone and iPad by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android by Google, under the Google Play refund policies.

One account at a time. A Chat+ subscription can be linked to only one Fellowship account at a time. If Chat+ doesn’t show on a new device, use Restore purchases in Fellowship.

Colophon Pro is separate. Colophon Pro is a one-time purchase: it is charged once and never renews. Chat+ doesn’t change it: if you own Colophon Pro, you keep every Pro feature whether or not you subscribe, and after Chat+ ends. Colophon Pro doesn’t include Chat+ or posting on the Prayer Wall.

When Chat+ ends, posting prayer requests stops, any daily message limits apply again, and Colophon Pro features close again unless you own Colophon Pro. Nothing you made with them is deleted: your notes, notebooks and study stay on your device.

Sunday Worship and Confession

Colophon Pro, and Chat+ while it is active, include two more features:

  • Sunday Worship is an hour of Scripture and prayer each Sunday, in twelve short parts at the time you choose. Its prayers and reflections are written once a week for everyone with the help of AI; every passage is the Berean Standard Bible, from the Bible in the app. It is a devotional aid: it is not a church service and doesn’t replace one, it is not a sacrament, and it is not pastoral advice. AI-written words can be imperfect, so read them with discernment — and if you can, worship with a local congregation too.
  • Confession is a private, guided time of prayer. What you write there stays on your device, held only in the app’s memory while the screen is open, and is never saved or sent anywhere — not to us and not to any AI. (The keyboard you type with, including dictation, belongs to your device’s operating system.) Confession doesn’t take the place of confession to a priest or pastor where your tradition has it; it can help you prepare.
